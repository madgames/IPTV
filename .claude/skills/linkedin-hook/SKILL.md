# LinkedIn Hook

Only the first ~2 lines (roughly 140 characters) show before the "…see more" fold. The hook's only job is to earn that click. Write it for that job.

## Process

1. Identify the post's single most surprising, contrarian, or valuable idea. The hook sells THAT, not the topic.
2. Draft 5 hooks using different angles:
   - **Result first:** "We cut churn 40% by deleting our onboarding emails."
   - **Contrarian:** "Most career advice about networking is backwards."
   - **Confession/story:** "I got fired from my first agency job. Best thing that ever happened."
   - **Specific number/fact:** "87% of the CVs I review die in the first 6 seconds."
   - **Direct challenge to the reader:** "Your pricing page is losing you deals you never see."
3. Present all 5, mark your pick, and say why in one line.
4. If the user shares the full post, also check the hook doesn't spoil the payoff — tease it instead.

## Rules

- Line 1 must work standalone; assume line 2 gets cut on mobile.
- Concrete beats clever: numbers, names, outcomes. No "Let's talk about X."
- No engagement-bait ("Agree?", "Thoughts?"), no "I'm humbled to announce", no emojis unless the user's existing posts use them.
- No em dashes, no AI-giveaway words (leverage, unlock, game-changer, journey).
- Match the user's actual voice if samples are available (see personal-voice skill).
